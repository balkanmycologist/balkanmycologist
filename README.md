### The Balkan Mycologist — Applied Mycology, Field Guides & Fungal Toxicology

Independent applied mycologist, field forager, and author based in the Balkans. My work focuses on regional wild mushroom macromorphology, look-alike safety diagnostics, post-harvest dehydration protocols, and the biochemistry of isoxazole-containing fungi.

  Regional identification keys for *Boletus edulis* and close allies across Europe, the Pacific Northwest, the East Coast, and the Rocky Mountains, paired with look-alike exclusions and exact dehydration temperatures.
